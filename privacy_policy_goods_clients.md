# Privacy Policy for Goods Client App

**Last updated: 25 April 2025**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Goods Client App ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your personal data. The App is developed and maintained by Mohamed Amer ("we", "our", or "the Service Provider") and is provided as a free service.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

When using the App, we may collect the following types of data:

- **Account information:** Name, email address, and phone number provided during registration.
- **Device information:** IP address, operating system, device model, and unique device identifiers.
- **Usage data:** Pages viewed, time spent, date/time of visits, and in-app actions.
- **Location data:** Approximate or precise geographic location via device GPS or network info (only when permission is granted).
- **Transaction data:** Order history, delivery addresses, and payment-related metadata (we do not store full card numbers).

---

## 2. Why We Collect This Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

- **App functionality:** Process orders, manage accounts, and deliver core features.
- **Location-based services:** Show relevant sellers, products, or delivery options based on your area.
- **Notifications:** Send order updates, delivery status, and important alerts via push notifications.
- **Analytics:** Understand how users interact with the App to improve performance and experience.
- **Security:** Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access.

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Delete** your account and all associated data.

## 6. Opt-Out

You can limit data collection in the following ways:

- **Location:** Disable location permissions in your device settings.
- **Notifications:** Disable push notifications in your device settings.
- **Full opt-out:** Uninstall the App to stop all data collection.
